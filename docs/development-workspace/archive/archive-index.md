# Engineering archive

The archive retains inactive workspace material when its history, evidence, or
reasoning may help later work. It is not a source of current system behaviour,
an active plan, or a decision awaiting implementation.

## Lifecycle boundary

Move material here when it is superseded, completed without a current-work
role, abandoned, or otherwise no longer actively maintained. Preserve its
status and context rather than rewriting it as current truth. A later effort
may cite an archived item as historical input, but must re-establish the
relevant evidence in a current investigation, decision, plan, or Project
Knowledge document.

## Relationship to other work

The archive is the endpoint for inactive material from the other Development
Workspace categories. Active follow-up belongs in
[[planning-index|Engineering planning]], unresolved questions in
[[investigations-index|Engineering investigations]], and accepted current
understanding in Project Knowledge.
