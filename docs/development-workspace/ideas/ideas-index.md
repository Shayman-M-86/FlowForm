# Engineering ideas

Engineering ideas holds early possibilities worth preserving before they have a
defined scope, evidence base, or commitment. It is deliberately low ceremony:
an idea may be incomplete, speculative, or ultimately discarded.

## Boundary

An idea is neither a decision nor an implementation plan. It must not claim
that a proposed design is current behaviour, and it should not become a
technical-debt record merely because it identifies a possible improvement.

## Lifecycle

Promote an idea to research when it needs evidence, to an investigation when it
poses a bounded question, or to planning after an agreed direction exists.
Discarded or superseded ideas can move to the archive when their history remains
useful; otherwise they need no artificial promotion.

```text
                   +--> research
idea --clarify---->+--> investigation
                   +--> planning (direction already agreed)
                   +--> archive / discard
```
